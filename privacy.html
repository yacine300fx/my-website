Privacy Policy - Yacine Glass Calculator

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Introduction

"Yacine Glass Calculator" is a calculator for glass sizes and prices. We respect your privacy, and this policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data we collect

The app does not collect any personal data. There is no account or sign-up, and we do not collect your name, email, location, photos or contacts.

The customer names, sizes and prices you enter stay on your device only and are never sent to any server.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data with any third party, because we do not hold it.

External links

The app may contain links that open outside it, such as the developer's Facebook page or WhatsApp. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from any user, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when needed. The "last updated" date at the top will change accordingly.

Contact
